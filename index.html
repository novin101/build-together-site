Questions

Remodeling FAQ

Straight answers to what homeowners in Los Angeles and Orange County ask us most. Don't see yours? Ask us in the estimate form.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Los Angeles or Orange County?+

It depends on size, layout changes and finishes. After a free in-home walkthrough you get a line-item estimate so you can see where every dollar goes and adjust before we start.

Which cities do you serve?+

In Orange County we work in Laguna Woods, Mission Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita, Irvine, Newport Beach, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel and Lake Forest. In the Los Angeles area we work in Pasadena, Woodland Hills, Calabasas, Encino, Tarzana, Reseda, Studio City and Bell Canyon.

Do you pull permits?+

Yes. We handle permits and inspections with each city for any work that requires them, from Irvine to Pasadena.

Do you install pavers, artificial turf and pergolas?+

Yes. Outside the home we build pergolas and patio covers, decks, paver patios and walkways, outdoor tile and artificial turf with proper base prep and drainage.

Can we live at home during the remodel?+

Usually, yes. We set up dust barriers, protect floors and plan the work so you keep a working bathroom and as much of your routine as possible.
